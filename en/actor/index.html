The actor

Stage actor with leading roles in television and film, award-winning director and producer.

Seen internationally in So Long, Marianne, the Leonard Cohen series, and in Greece in Stelios, one of the biggest box-office hits in Greek cinema. On stage at the Greek Art Theatre Karolos Koun, the Municipal Theatre of Piraeus and the Municipal Regional Theatre of Crete.

Founder of Birigogo, and a voice heard in more than 10,000 voice-overs.
