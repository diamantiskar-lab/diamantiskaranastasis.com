Cinema

«The basic essential of a great actor is that he loves himself in acting»Charlie Chaplin

I have starred in short and feature films.

My first feature film direction “My Blood” (also produced, screenplay writing and editing) achieved a total of 9 awards and multiple honours at festivals worldwide, including one nomination and one award at Greek Film Academy Awards.

The film is a completely independent production. In the film I also play one of the two leading male parts.
