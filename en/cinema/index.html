Cinema

«The basic essential of a great actor is that he loves himself in acting»Charlie Chaplin

I have acted in, directed and produced both feature and short films. Among others, I played the lead in Vassilis Raisis’ “Timeless Stories” (2017), winner of the Audience Award at the 58th Thessaloniki International Film Festival. In Giorgos Tsemberopoulos’ biographical film “Stelios” (2024) I portrayed Manolis Chiotis, a major figure in Greek folk music and partner of Stelios Kazantzidis, in a film that became one of the highest-grossing Greek films of all time.

My debut feature as director, “My Blood” (“Aima”, 2011), which I also wrote, produced, edited and starred in, is an independent, no-budget parable based on the Oedipus myth. It screened at festivals in the United States, the United Kingdom, Indonesia, India, Croatia, Spain and the Netherlands, winning nine international awards, and received a nomination and a win at the Hellenic Film Academy Awards.

During the 2021 lockdown I directed “Pyramides” (“Pyramids”) by Andreas Flourakis for the Karolos Koun Art Theatre, the first fully digital Greek theatre production made for online audiences. Critic Dr. Konstantinos Bouras, writing in To Vima, proposed a special award for its cinematography and editing.
