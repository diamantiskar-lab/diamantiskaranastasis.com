Biography

«Art is a lie that makes us realize truth, at least the truth that is given us to understand.»Pablo Picasso

I was born in Larissa, Greece. I studied Computer Engineering at the National Polytechnic University of Athens. During my studies there, I followed my passion and started my drama education.

I work in Theatre, TV and Cinema since 1998 and performed in critically acclaimed stage productions and popular Greek TV series.

My first feature film direction “My Blood” (also produced, screenplay writing and editing) achieved a total of 9 awards and multiple honours at festivals worldwide, including one nomination and one award at Greek Film Academy Awards.

My voice has been featured in over 10,000 advertisements, for Greek and International companies as well as in documentaries, narrations and Greek adaptations of blockbuster animation films.

In my own post production studio DACOS I have created hundreds of multimedia productions, video art and promotional videos and audios.

«The starting point and basis of theatre, as in every art form, is poetry and magic.»Karolos Koun
